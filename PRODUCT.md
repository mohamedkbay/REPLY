# Reply

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

An Arabic-speaking shop owner managing products and automatic customer replies from a computer or phone.

## Product Purpose

Customers send a product code and receive its current name, price and optional photo. The owner keeps a simple inventory and controls which products are available.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Arabic RTL by default, with a complete English LTR administration interface. Select language at the top left of sign-in; the choice persists across navigation. Saved customer replies and product data keep their original wording.
- Local Cairo in both languages; all four weights are preloaded, font rendering blocks a visible fallback swap, and text motion begins after the faces load. Top navigation adapts to phones.
- Product codes, photos, prices, currency selection (default Libyan dinar), availability and search.
- Unavailable products receive an apology; unknown codes prompt the customer to check their code.
- Account connections and automatic reply configuration belong in settings.
- TikTok comment automation is a priority. Real connections require official credentials and permissions; local operation currently uses dry-run mode. TikTok's current comment preview is text-only.
- Existing routes, forms, authentication and reply behavior must survive visual changes.

## Brand Commitments

Reply is the confirmed single-word name. The user rejected the previous faint interface and three palette previews, and supplied https://afternow.co/ as the visual reference for the replacement. The user explicitly prioritizes animation, motion and style, rejected the first monochrome table-only adaptation as unrelated, and requested applying the corrected design to every page with a tidy phone layout. Follow the reference’s layered full-screen navigation, strong image panels, expressive space and motion while preserving the usable Arabic administration interface.

The new response R monogram and outlined Reply wordmark are adopted. Use the logo at sign-in and the shared header; avoid repeating it throughout sections and the footer. Keep pattern accents bounded to sign-in and menu feature panels. The complete editable assets and Arabic project guide are delivered in Reply-Brand-Kit.

## Evidence on Hand

The local database contains explicitly labelled demonstration products, including a backpack with a photo. No social account is connected. Do not invent message counts, connection status, testimonials or results.

## Product Principles

- Keep product entry and availability control very simple.
- Make sections visually distinct and easy to find.
- Use the actual inventory as the source of price replies.
- Preserve clear Arabic labels and honest operating status.
